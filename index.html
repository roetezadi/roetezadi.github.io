About Me

I am Romina Etezadi, a PhD candidate at the University of Ottawa, Canada. My PhD is under the supervision of Lionel Briand, and my co-advisors is Chetan Arora. I am a member of the Nanda Lab in the faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS). I am interested in conducting research related to Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) and exploring its practical applications in industry.

Outside of work, I enjoy biking, going to the gym, reading books (check out some of my favorite reads here!).

Research Interests
